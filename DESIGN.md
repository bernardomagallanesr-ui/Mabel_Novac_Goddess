## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a visual language centered on haute editorial luxury merged with high-velocity digital utility. It serves an elite, multi-category salon ecosystem covering master barbering, couture hair styling, restorative scalp therapy, and family beauty management.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Aesthetic Position:** Non-gendered atelier luxury. Avoids hyper-feminine pastel tropes and aggressive masculine industrialism in favor of architectural poise, dark obsidian depth, and polished champagne brilliance.
- **Audience:** Discerning clientele expecting bespoke concierge hospitality, alongside professional atelier stylists requiring precise schedule management and high-density service data.
- **Emotional Response:** Indulgence, clinical precision, calm authority, and seamless operational mastery.

### Design Movement
A structured fusion of **Modern Editorialism** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- High-contrast typography borrowing from luxury print publications.
- Translucent, satin-blurred backing cards layered over obsidian and warm platinum-tinted surfaces.
- Micro-fine hairline borders evoking fine metalwork, jewel facets, and tailored craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette balances radiant metallurgical warmth with grounded obsidian contrast and delicate rose accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Champagne & Industrial Gold - `#C59B27`, `#D4AF37`, `#E6CA65`):** Serves as the primary luxury signifier. Used for key interaction anchors, selected states, elevated badges, active tier indicators, and refined micro-accents.
- **Secondary (Dusty Ruby / Atelier Wine - `#9E2A3B`, `#B83A4B`, tint `#FDF2F4`):** Expresses VIP treatments, special allocations, priority bookings, and high-touch alert messaging without resorting to alarmist visual styles.
- **Tertiary (Warm Champagne Shimmer - `#E6CA65`):** Used for micro-highlights, glowing hover states, and delicate active outlines.
- **Neutral & Obsidian Base (`#121113`, `#1F1D20`, `#2C282D`):** The primary anchor for deep typography, contrast fills, dark-mode elevated surfaces, and confident framing.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#FAFAFA`, `#F5F3F4`):** Soft warm ivory and crisp alabaster canvas tones that eliminate stark, sterile white spaces.
- **Structural Platinum Hairlines (`#E8E4DB`, `#D9D3C7` with alpha):** Micro-borders infused with warm gold-tinted undertones to replace generic gray divides.

## Typography
The typographical tension between **Playfair Display** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** reflects the platform's union of high fashion and streamlined utility.

### Hierarchy & Rules
- **Playfair Display:** Reserved for brand statements, editorial hero introductions, service section headers, and stylist profile names. Never set below `20px` to maintain serif integrity.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives booking schedules, seat matrices, pricing tiers, forms, metadata tags, and confirmation flows. Clean geometry prevents optical fatigue during dense data scanning.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase micro-labels and status indicators use deliberate tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to mimic couture brand signatures.
- **Number Formats:** All monetary values and appointment timestamps in tables or schedules use proportional lining numerals via Plus Jakarta Sans for frictionless vertical visual scanning.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a balanced 12-column architectural grid on desktop, converting to an 8-column layout on tablet and a single fluid column on mobile.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max-width of `1440px` centered on canvas. Wide gutters (`1.5rem`) ensure elevated breathing space between booking modules, calendar inspectors, and stylist portfolios.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical sections leverage `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to reinforce an unhurried, luxury salon atmosphere. Micro-interactions within booking drawers and slot pickers scale strictly with `space-xs` through `space-md`.
- **Reflow Architecture:**
  - *Desktop:* Multi-pane operational split (Left: Concierge service selector; Center: Interactive seat/stylist schedule; Right: Dynamic reservation drawer).
  - *Tablet:* Tabbed split-view; persistent collapsible summary bar.
  - *Mobile:* Stacked progressive flow. Fixed bottom sheet for scheduling steps, sticky pricing, and one-tap checkout.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and glassmorphic surface tiers.

### Stratification Model
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Untinted Alabaster (`#FAFAFA`) or Midnight Obsidian (`#121113`) background.
2. **Level 1 (Atelier Cards & Partitions):** Semi-opaque matte surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` in light mode, `rgba(31, 29, 32, 0.78)` in dark mode) backed by a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and bounded by a `1px` platinum-gold hairline (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`).
3. **Level 2 (Active Appointment Drawers & Flyouts):** Suspended glass surfaces with a dual-shadow scheme:
   - Ambient: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(18, 17, 19, 0.07)`
   - Rim: `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`
4. **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & Toast Notifications):** Floating translucent layers with `24px` backdrop blur, anchored by a deep diffused shadow: `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(18, 17, 19, 0.18)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy adopts **Soft (`1`)** corner geometry. This preserves tailored architectural precision without the aggressive sharpness of brutalism or the playful informality of fully rounded pill interfaces.

### Curvature Values
- **Micro Components (Checkboxes, mini tags, badges):** `2px` to `4px` corner radii.
- **Core Interactive Elements (Inputs, buttons, dropdown triggers):** `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- **Cards & Surface Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Overlays, Drawers & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- **Exceptions:** Avatar portraits and stylist credentials utilize a strict circle (`9999px`) or a crisp square with a `4px` bevel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action:** Obsidian black background (`#1F1D20`), champagne gold hairline border (`#D4AF37`), pure white text, uppercase label tracking (`0.06em`). On hover, transitions to subtle champagne fill (`#C59B27`) with dark text and a warm ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Atelier:** Transparent fill, `1px` border in platinum-gold (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.4)`), `#1F1D20` text. Hover prompts background transition to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive / Cancellation:** Dusty ruby hairline (`#9E2A3B`), ruby text, shifts to soft rose fill (`#FDF2F4`) on hover.

### Booking & Time Chips
- **Default Slot:** Clean white surface, `1px` border (`#E8E4DB`), `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium text.
- **Selected Slot:** Solid Obsidian (`#1F1D20`) or Champagne Gold (`#C59B27`) fill, white text, micro checkmark indicator.
- **Unavailable / Waitlist:** Dimmed opacity (`40%`), slash pattern or subdued strike-through, disabled pointer.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Translucent ivory-white (`#FFFFFF`).
- Resting State: `1px` muted platinum border (`#D9D3C7`).
- Active / Focus State: Hairline shifts to vibrant champagne (`#D4AF37`) with a subtle `2px` focus halo (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- Typography: Floating labels styled with `label-sm` tracking.

### Cards (Service & Stylist Profiles)
- Glassmorphic backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline gold border (`#E8E4DB`).
- Incorporates high-aspect imagery, category tags (e.g., "HAUTE COUTURE", "EXECUTIVE BARBER", "SCALP RITUAL"), service durations in crisp lining numerals, and explicit direct-booking action triggers.

### Status Badges
- **Confirmed / VIP:** Soft champagne wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`) with `#967417` text and a fine gold dot.
- **Priority / Special Request:** Dusty ruby tint (`#FDF2F4`) with `#9E2A3B` typography.
- **In-Chair / Active Session:** Obsidian fill with gold text and pulsing status beacon.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with soft `2px` radius (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- Gold accent fills on check state with white checkmark glyph. Never use default OS browser styles.
